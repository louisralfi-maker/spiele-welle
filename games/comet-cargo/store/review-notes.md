# Comet Cargo: notes for App Review

**How to play.** Tap the big "Launch" button in the hangar, then tap anywhere on the launch pad to ignite. During the
flight, tap anywhere on the screen to drop the current rocket stage; the best moment is when the tank gauge on the left
is almost empty (the "Drop!" tag next to the rocket marks it). When the rocket passes through a glowing orbit ring, tap
the round satellite button at the bottom right to release a satellite into that ring. Higher rings pay more coins;
satellites still aboard at the highest point of the flight are lost. After the flight report, tap "Collect" and spend
the coins on the five upgrades in the hangar (engines, tanks, stages, cargo bay, guidance) to fly higher. A flight lasts
20 to 90 seconds. The first flights show a tapping hand that explains each step.

**Account.** There is no login, no account and no sign-up. Progress is stored only on the device.

**Purchases.** The app has no in-app purchases.

**Ads.** Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK):

- an interstitial, shown rarely and only after a flight report when the player taps "Collect" (never during the first
  minutes of play or the first flights);
- one optional rewarded ad: "Double it" on the flight report doubles that flight's coins once.

Consent for ads uses Google's User Messaging Platform where the regulations require it, followed by the App Tracking
Transparency prompt. Players can change their ad privacy choices at any time under Settings → "Privacy choices" (shown
where the regulations require it). The privacy policy is linked in Settings → "Privacy policy".

**Other.** No music; sound effects and haptics only, both can be switched off in Settings. The app collects no personal
data of its own.
