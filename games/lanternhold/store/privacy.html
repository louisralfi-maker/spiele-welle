Privacy Policy

Lanternhold for iPhone · Last updated: 14 November 2026

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, no analytics and no servers of our own. Your realm is saved only on your iPhone, and the name you give your lord or lady never leaves it.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. To show them, Google's advertising software collects some data from your device (section 3). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks you first. On iPhone you also decide whether apps may track you.
  • Questions: louis.ralf@web.de

1. Who is responsible

Louis Herwick
Germany

"We" in this policy means Louis Herwick, the independent developer of Lanternhold.

2. What stays on your device

  • Your saved realm (the day, what you built, your people, your gold and the decisions you made), the name and title you chose for your lord or lady, your settings (sound and haptics) and the time of the last ad (so that ads stay rare) are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone.
  • The name you type is used only on screen and in that save. It is never sent anywhere, not with an ad request either.
  • We do not receive this data and cannot see it. It disappears when you delete the app. It can be part of your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) if you use backups; that is controlled by you and Apple.
  • The game does not ask for an account, e-mail address, contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

3. Advertising with Google AdMob

Lanternhold is free and is financed by ads served through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). There are two kinds:

  • short videos that you choose to watch for a reward named on the button, such as double taxes in a dawn report or a second chance when the realm falls;
  • now and then a full-screen ad after you have read a dawn report, never in the first days of a new realm, never at night, during an audience or in a story scene.

There are no banner ads. We ask Google to show only ads suitable for a general audience.

When an ad is requested or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app and Google may collect and process:

  • your IP address, from which Google may estimate the approximate location of your device (the game never asks for your location);
  • device and software information, such as device model, iOS version, language and screen size;
  • the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allowed tracking in the iOS prompt; otherwise other identifiers that are limited to this app or developer;
  • information about ads and your interaction with them, such as ads requested, shown, watched or tapped, and app launches and taps in connection with ads;
  • diagnostic and performance data, such as crash logs, app launch time, hangs and energy use.

Google uses this data to choose, deliver and measure ads, to prevent fraud and abuse, and to improve its services. Google processes the data under its own responsibility according to Google's Privacy Policy; more on how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We only receive reports in aggregated form (for example the number of ads shown and the revenue). We cannot identify you from them.

Your choices

  • Consent form (EEA, UK and Switzerland): when the game starts, Google's consent software (the Google User Messaging Platform) asks Google whether a consent form is needed where you are. This sends your IP address and basic device information to Google; no ad is requested. Where the form is needed, it appears at your first dawn report, before any ad is requested, and asks for your choices about ad personalisation and about storing and reading information on your device. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the game's settings under "Privacy choices" (shown wherever the form is required). Without consent you may still see ads, but they are not personalised.
  • iOS tracking prompt: after the consent form, iOS asks whether the game may track you across apps and websites of other companies. If you tap "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised with it. You can change this at any time in iOS under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Google ad settings: if you use a Google account, you can manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.

Legal basis. Where the consent form is shown, your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and section 25(1) of the German TDDDG). For the check whether a form is needed, and elsewhere, our legitimate interest in financing the free game and in asking for consent where the law requires it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and, for Google's own processing, the legal bases stated by Google.

4. Purchases and Apple

The game has no in-app purchases. From Apple we receive aggregated download reports, not personal data. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. What we do not do

  • no accounts or logins, and no data about you on our own servers (we have none);
  • no analytics, profiling or tracking of our own;
  • no selling of personal data and no social-media or other third-party log-in.

6. When you write to us

If you e-mail us, we process your e-mail address and your message to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We delete them once your request is dealt with and no legal duty to keep them applies.

7. Recipients and transfers to other countries

Recipients of the advertising data are Google (Google Ireland Limited, Dublin, for users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland; Google LLC, USA, elsewhere) and the ad partners Google works with. Google may process data in the USA and other countries. For transfers from the EEA, Google relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and on standard contractual clauses.

8. How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete the app. For data held by Google, Google's retention rules apply (see its privacy policy).

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and you can withdraw a consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR). Because we hold almost no personal data about players, requests mostly concern e-mails you sent us. For data processed by Google, you can also use Google's tools and contact points (see its privacy policy). Write to louis.ralf@web.de.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one where you live or work, or the one competent for us in Germany.

10. Children

Lanternhold is a game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. In the EEA the age at which a child can give valid consent is set by national law (between 13 and 16); younger players should ask a parent or guardian before answering the consent form. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

11. US residents

We do not sell personal information ourselves. Showing personalised ads through Google can count as "sharing" or "sale" under some US state privacy laws. You can opt out by tapping "Ask App Not to Track" in the iOS prompt or by changing the choice later under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, and through Google's ad settings above.

12. Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects your data (for example new advertising partners), we update this page and its date. The current version is always at the address you opened.

3. Werbung mit Google AdMob

Lanternhold ist kostenlos und finanziert sich durch Werbung über Google AdMob (das Google Mobile Ads SDK). Es gibt zwei Arten:

  • kurze Videos, die du freiwillig ansiehst, um eine Belohnung zu erhalten, die auf der Schaltfläche steht, etwa doppelte Steuern im Morgenbericht oder eine zweite Chance, wenn das Reich fällt;

Banner gibt es nicht. Wir bitten Google, nur Werbung zu zeigen, die für ein allgemeines Publikum geeignet ist.

Wenn Werbung angefordert oder angezeigt wird, können das Google Mobile Ads SDK in der App und Google Folgendes erfassen und verarbeiten:

  • deine IP-Adresse, aus der Google den ungefähren Standort deines Geräts schätzen kann (das Spiel fragt nie nach deinem Standort);
  • Geräte- und Softwareinformationen, etwa Gerätemodell, iOS-Version, Sprache und Bildschirmgröße;
  • die Werbe-ID (IDFA), wenn du das Tracking in der iOS-Abfrage erlaubt hast; sonst andere Kennungen, die auf diese App oder diesen Entwickler beschränkt sind;
  • Angaben zu Werbung und deiner Interaktion damit, etwa angeforderte, angezeigte, angesehene oder angetippte Anzeigen sowie App-Starts und Tipps im Zusammenhang mit Werbung;
  • Diagnose- und Leistungsdaten, etwa Absturzprotokolle, Startzeit der App, Hänger und Energieverbrauch.

Google nutzt diese Daten, um Werbung auszuwählen, auszuliefern und zu messen, um Betrug und Missbrauch zu verhindern und um seine Dienste zu verbessern. Google verarbeitet die Daten in eigener Verantwortung nach der Datenschutzerklärung von Google; mehr dazu, wie Google Informationen aus Apps nutzt, die seine Dienste verwenden: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Wir erhalten nur zusammengefasste Berichte (zum Beispiel die Zahl der gezeigten Anzeigen und die Einnahmen). Daraus können wir dich nicht erkennen.
