# childA 12x18, 12 frames: run 0-3, fidget 4-5, skip 6-9, the stick raised 10 and struck 11 (docs/SPRITES-people.md,
# the children's contract; 12 wide from people-2, so the swung stick fits the frame). Nib Brisk, 7, Wenna's youngest
# (docs/PEOPLE.md). Quick where everyone else is slow, and the bright thing in a worn town: a mop of straw hair, a
# brother's linen tunic far too big for him (the sleeves over his hands, the hem to his knees), washed white by his
# mother at the well, a step lighter than any grown-up's cloth; bare feet, a stick for a sword; and the warm clean
# accent that belongs to the children: a red kerchief tied round the back of his neck, its knot and ends a small clean
# triangle on his chest, the throat under the chin left white (the director, 15.11.: nothing on a child may read as
# blood at phone size, so no red on the throat or the belly), and a small square grey patch with a dark seam on the
# tunic at the knee. The tunic's shade is cool (`mistLight`), so the white stands off
# the cream plaster of the houses. Light from the upper left; look 2's selective outline. Run: contact, flight,
# contact, flight (the body up 1 px and the feet off the ground in flight), the stick held high. Fidget: on both feet,
# then on one foot rubbing the other against his shin. Skip: the step and the hop on each foot (2 px up on the hop),
# the arms thrown up. The fight (with Wren, `TownDay`): feet planted wide, the stick raised back over his head (10),
# then thrust level before him in a lunge (11); the stick three pixels long in both, so it never changes length
