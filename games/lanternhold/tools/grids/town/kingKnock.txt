# kingKnock 16x30, 3 frames (town-2, `TownSprites.KnockFrame`): the Burgherr at a door of the town, demanding more
# (docs/TOWN.md: "Wenn man nachfordert und auf die Tür drückt, klopft der König dran"). His body is the walking
# sprite's standing frame (../actors/king.txt frame 6, the same anchor), and the lantern pole stays exactly where that
# frame holds it, in his near hand before him, so nothing jumps when the pose starts or ends and his glow strip
# (kingGlow frame 6) and the lantern's light stay on the glass (fix round 1: the pole no longer swaps to his back).
# He knocks with his free far hand: it comes out under his near arm, passes behind the pole and raps the door at the
# height of his hip, where the town's low doors are; then it is held out, palm up, for the coins.
#   0 the fist drawn back, 1 the knock (the fist on the door ahead of him), 2 the open hand held out for the coins
