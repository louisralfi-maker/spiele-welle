# lodge 56x44, hand-set: the builders' lodge: a timber-framed workshop of vertical boards on a low stone base, braces,
# a window, the big doors open (a saw on the wall inside), a shingle roof with a chimney, a saw sign, stacked logs, a
# ladder and a wheelbarrow.
