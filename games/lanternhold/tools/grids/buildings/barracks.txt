# barracks 64x48, hand-set: a long two-storey hall: a stone ground floor, a dark timber upper storey on a jetty with
# four windows (dark glass), a teal slate roof, an arched oak door under a teal banner with the gold lantern, two wall
# lanterns (dark glass), spear racks and round shields either side.
