# Lanternhold

You are the king of a castle that seals a mountain gorge. By day you build walls, towers and fires, walk your halls and
judge your people's petitions from the throne while the town behind the castle lives in peace. By night the Gloom, a
living darkness whose creatures shrink from light, storms the gorge, and arrows and fire hold it back until dawn.
Landscape, side view, pixel art; warm fire against a cold night.
